# Episode 2 — Chapter 11: "The Quadratic Funding round"

A secret Keeper meeting in a sealed booth. The Keepers are talking about funding open air-filter tech when one of them explains why Quadratic Funding is "a mess right now": a program paid people to donate **10 zipcoins** and get **20** back, while the project got **100** with matching. It turns out Bluewhale was behind it.

Length: ~75–85 seconds.

---

## Dialogue (word for word from Snowmoon, chapter 11)

One unbroken stretch of the chapter, nothing added or reordered. The Keepers are anonymous, so they're labelled A, B and C here.

1. **Keeper B:** "Increase the maximum to two thirds of a percent. And then make the case to the funding organs - Graph Funding, Quadratic Funding, parliament allocations - that they should support more open-"
2. **Keeper C:** "Quadratic funding? That round is a mess right now. Someone released a program you can download onto your device and prove that you donated to a particular project researching social media polarization - actually not a very good one - and get a reward. You donate ten zipcoins and get back twenty, they get a hundred including the bounded matching. Lots of people participated. And then yesterday some insider at Bluewhale blew the whistle, apparently that was all their doing."
3. **Keeper A:** "Bluewhale? But why would they do that? They don't even control the project that was getting that funding."
4. **Keeper C:** "It doesn't matter where it goes, if it was hiring people from Iptak to come dig holes and then fill them back up again they would still have done it. Their goal is to drain funding from anything that interfaces with open platforms."
5. **Keeper B:** "Fine, we'll stick to Graph Funding. Go through our channels to suggest to the Graph Funding people that they support more open source tech for air filtering, cleaning, wastewater scanning, all of it."

Watch reading at the start (from the book): **CO2 906 → 918, PM2.5 4.4**.
