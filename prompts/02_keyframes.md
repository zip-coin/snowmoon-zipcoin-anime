# 2. Keyframes (still images)

Each keyframe is the first frame of a video clip. Made in Grok / ChatGPT with the character sheets attached. Results are in `images/keyframes/`.

Every prompt starts with a line telling the model to keep the references the same. In this scene Gladias always has the hood DOWN and the neck band visible.

Screen-direction rule for the dialogue: Gladias is on the LEFT of the frame and faces RIGHT, and Febric is on the RIGHT and faces LEFT.

---
