## Gladias

```
Create an anime character reference sheet on a plain white background.

Character: Gladias, a 39-year-old man from Veridia. Calm, thoughtful, a bit of a quiet philosopher, with a hint of mischief. Average build.
Face and hair: short dark brown hair, dark eyes, light brown skin, clean-shaven, soft kind face

Outfit (keep exactly the same in every view):
- Veridian privacy robe: loose-fitting, full-length, dark purple robe covering the body from a hood down to the ankles
- A front face cover attached to the robe, with a strap across the face that separates the area above the nose from below it
- Dark purple shoes that match the robe, with raised heels and slightly uneven soles
- A tight silky black cloth band around his neck (a whisper mic)
- A small earpiece in one ear
- A smart watch on his left wrist
- A slim handheld device (like a phone) in one hand

Layout:
- Top row: full-body front view, side view and back view, hood DOWN, face cover off, face visible
- Second row: full-body front view with hood UP and face cover ON
- Bottom row: 3 face close-ups (neutral, slightly embarrassed smile, focused)
- Close-up detail boxes of the watch, the neck band and the shoes

Style: clean cel-shaded anime, soft muted colours, consistent line weight, same proportions in every view. No text labels on the character, no background scenery.
```

Follow-up fix (he looked too young):
```
Make him look late 30s, faint lines around the eyes, slightly more mature face, keep everything else identical.
```
